Design process

Much more than what meets the eye.

I work collaboratively with my team across the whole end-to-end process of product development — from the first messy question to what the final metrics say post-launch.

01

Discover

Explore the problem deeply before landing on a solution. Understand the needs of the users and the business.

02

Ideate

Work through multiple ideas with flows, wireframes, and prototypes, then narrow down through critique and quick validation.

03

Build

Partner with engineering through build and QA, covering the edge cases and empty states along the way.

04

Measure

Track adoption and success metrics after release, then use what the data shows to prioritize the next round of work.
